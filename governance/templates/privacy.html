Data Governance and Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Shadow AI monitors and logs incoming prompt metadata to enforce corporate data security standards and model access governance.

1. Information Logged

Our interceptor records the user identifier, target AI model, submitted data sensitivity tier, and execution enforcement decisions (Approved or Blocked).

2. Purpose of Processing

Audit records are maintained exclusively for compliance verification, unauthorized model invocation prevention, and corporate security governance.
