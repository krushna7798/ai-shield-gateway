Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Data Interception and Logging

The Shadow AI gateway actively intercepts API payloads submitted by authorized personnel. All data payloads are scanned by the Data Loss Prevention engine to assign a compliance tier. Metadata regarding the request, including the submitter identifier and assigned data tier, is immutably logged in the system database.

2. Retention of Sensitive Information

The system does not permanently store the raw text of intercepted Tier 3 payloads. Only the compliance violation event, timestamp, and target model are retained for auditing and regulatory reporting purposes.

3. Third-Party Processors

Data classified as Tier 1 or Tier 2 is forwarded to approved third-party vendors in accordance with the active Model Inventory rules. Data classified as Tier 3 is blocked locally and never transmitted to external networks.
